What’s included

Everything a practice needs. Nothing it has to maintain.

A dashboard, not a dependency

Log in, edit a page, save. Hours, bios, insurance lists and photos stay current because the staff can change them in seconds.

Built for the phone first

Most patients find a practice on a phone in a parking lot. Every layout is drawn for that screen before any other.

Nothing to break at 2am

Pages are prerendered to flat files. No server to crash, no database to restore, no cold start between a patient and your phone number.
