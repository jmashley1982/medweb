South Texas Vascular Experts

Comprehensive Vascular Services

From diagnosis to recovery, we offer a full spectrum of vascular treatments tailored to you.

Carotid Artery Disease Management

Stroke prevention through advanced imaging and carotid endarterectomy or stenting procedures.

Peripheral Arterial Disease (PAD)

Restoring blood flow to the legs with angioplasty, stenting, and bypass surgery.

Varicose Vein Treatment

Sclerotherapy and laser ablation to eliminate painful, unsightly varicose and spider veins.

Dialysis Access

Creation and maintenance of arteriovenous fistulas and grafts for dialysis patients.

Aneurysm Repair

Endovascular and open surgical repair of abdominal and thoracic aortic aneurysms.

Wound Care

Specialized wound care for diabetic ulcers, venous stasis wounds, and post-surgical healing.

Every patient is unique, which is why every treatment plan at South Texas Vascular Experts is built around your specific condition, lifestyle, and goals. We use the latest minimally invasive approaches whenever possible, meaning smaller incisions, faster recovery, and better outcomes.
